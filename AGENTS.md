### Skill: Software quality analysis
Use when reviewing SonarQube results, quality issues or technical debt.
1. Read QUALITY_PLAN.md.
2. Inspect issue, rule and affected code.
3. Explain why the issue appears.
4. Evaluate its real impact.
5. Relate it to quality objectives and debt.
6. Propose alternative treatments.
7. Identify risks and regression tests.
8. Recommend an action.
9. Wait for human approval.
